# TASK-015: Challenges, Tournaments, and Community Play

**Status:** Idea

## Goal

Turn experimentation into a repeatable game.

## Candidate modes

- Predict the winner before running
- Daily seeded scenario
- Built-in method tournament
- Custom-method tournament
- Beat Steffen with three rules or fewer
- Keep families together under maximum chaos
- Minimize total time without exceeding a fairness threshold
- "What changed?" mystery scenario

## Scoring

Scores may combine completion time, passenger experience, rule complexity, and policy compliance. Every scoring formula must be visible.

## Sharing

Users can share scenario links, custom methods, and result cards. A serverless initial version can encode everything in URLs or downloadable JSON rather than requiring accounts.

## Community caution

A public leaderboard requires abuse prevention, version locking, and reproducible verification. It should not precede stable scenario and method schemas.

## Acceptance criteria

- At least one challenge has a clear success condition
- Runs are seed- and version-reproducible
- Scoring is transparent
- Fun presentation does not change simulation mechanics
- Community features are optional and privacy-conscious
